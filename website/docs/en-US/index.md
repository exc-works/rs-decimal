# rs-decimal

rs-decimal is a Rust library for working with decimal numbers. Use it to add prices, multiply a unit price by a quantity, or choose how many decimal places to print on a receipt.

## What goes on the receipt?

Two notebooks at 4.50 each, plus 1.25 for delivery. The customer pays 10.25. With Decimal, the same numbers can go from the order form to the calculation and onto the receipt.

<div class="receipt-equation">4.50 × 2 + 1.25 = <strong>10.25</strong></div>
